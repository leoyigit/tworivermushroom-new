Our broth is launching at Sprouts Farmers Market — all 465 stores

Two River Mushroom Broth is going national. Our flagship Lion’s Mane broth — winner of a sofi™ Gold Award and a Retailers’ Choice Award — is rolling into the innovation aisle at every Sprouts Farmers Market in the country at $8.99.

Two more varieties follow later this year: our wild-harvested Chaga broth and a Thai-inspired Maitake coconut-milk broth. All three are shelf-stable, plant-based alternatives to bone broth, brewed from organic mushrooms grown on our farm plus about 20 pantry-simple ingredients.

“We saw an opportunity to expand into functional innovation by creating a first-to-market broth line,” says co-owner Kurt Cavano. The broth project began in 2021 with a USDA Value-Added Producer Grant — and grew out of a decade of supplying more than 75 restaurants and retailers across the Tri-State area.

Chef and brand ambassador Derek Sarno puts it more simply: the flavor is “deep, rich” — made for sipping straight or cooking with.
