Our story

“For those who desire only the finest gourmet mushrooms.”

— KC Sullivan, Founder

Two River Gourmet Mushrooms is a small-scale farm on Mighty Dare Farm — a 25-acre parcel of preserved farmland in historic Millstone, New Jersey. We grow high-quality USDA Organic mushrooms for those who desire only the finest.

We’re New Jersey’s first organic mushroom farm and, to date, the only USDA Organic mushroom farm in the state growing this range of specialty strains. Today we supply 40+ of the area’s finest restaurants and local farmers’ markets — and home cooks who stop by the farm.
