Frequently asked

Pick-up, shipping, subscriptions, and visiting the farm — the answers we give most often.

Fresh mushrooms & pick-up

Do you ship fresh mushrooms?

No — and that’s on purpose. To protect quality, we never ship perishables. Choose pick-up at checkout and collect your order at Mighty Dare Farm during open hours (Mon–Sat, 10AM–4PM). You’ll get an email with directions after checkout.

How fresh is “fresh”?

Most orders are picked by hand the same morning you collect them. If something isn’t right with your order, tell us within 24 hours of pick-up and we’ll replace it or refund you.

Where else can I buy your mushrooms?

We’re on the menus and shelves of 40+ restaurants, grocers and farmers’ markets across New Jersey. See the Where to Buy page.

Broth & shipping

Does the broth ship nationwide?

Yes — the broth is shelf-stable and ships free anywhere in the US. It’s the only thing we ship; everything fresh is farm pick-up only.

What’s actually in it?

Organic mushrooms from our farm — Lion’s Mane & Shiitake, Maitake, or Chaga depending on the flavor — plus about 20 pantry-simple ingredients: vegetables, herbs, aromatics. No fillers, no mystery powders. It won a sofi™ Gold in 2023 and a NEXTY in 2026.

Can I return broth or merchandise?

Unopened, shelf-stable broth and unworn merchandise can be returned within 14 days for a full refund — just bring your receipt to the farm. Details on the Returns page.

Subscriptions

How does Subscribe & Save work?

Pick a flavor, choose delivery every 2, 4 or 8 weeks, and save 10% on every box with free shipping. Manage everything yourself — pause, skip, swap flavors or cancel anytime.

What’s the Starter Box?

All three flavors in one box, so you can find your favorite. If you subscribe with a Starter Box, it automatically switches to the flavor you love from the second delivery — or stays mixed if you prefer.

Visiting the farm

Can I visit Mighty Dare Farm?

Yes! The pick-up barn at 145 Stillhouse Road, Millstone NJ is open Monday–Saturday, 10AM–4PM. Come by for the day’s harvest, broth, and merch.

Do you supply restaurants?

We supply 40+ of NJ’s finest kitchens. If you’re a chef or buyer, get in touch — we’d love to talk.
